[[James Ratsasane - Bio]]

Presentation length: 20 minutes

How do you scale the quality of design decisions when you're no longer in the room?  
  
In design leadership, the decisions you're responsible for shift from execution and experience quality to team capability, trade-offs, and investment scale. The challenge becomes creating an environment where good design decisions happen consistently without you.  
  
James Ratsasane, Head of Product & Design at MECCA, explores four parts of that environment: sensemaking rituals that help teams work at the right altitude; shared language across design, product, and engineering that makes trade-offs visible; experimentation to test customer hypotheses against live signal; and safeguards that help teams distinguish between reversible choices and high-consequence decisions.  
This is not about frameworks. It's about what happens when you get the conditions right: your team develops the judgment, the shared reasoning, and the trust to land great design decisions without you.

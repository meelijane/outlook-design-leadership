[[Sadia Mir - Bio]]

Presentation length: 30 mins

AI is not coming for design — but it is fundamentally changing what great design leadership looks like. Drawing on her experience leading human-centred designers at CSIRO's Data61, one of Australia's foremost AI research organisations, Sadia Mir has had a front-row seat to the ways AI is reshaping how we work, what we prioritise, and where our influence as leaders must grow. In this talk, she shares three pivotal shifts she believes every design leader needs to make — from expanding our role in ethical decision-making, to evolving how we define and measure human value in an AI-accelerated world. This is not a talk about tools. It is a call to step into the fuller version of what design leadership can be.

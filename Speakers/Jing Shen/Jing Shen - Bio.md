Jing Shen is an Assistant Director at IP Australia and a strategic service designer and design leader with experience spanning government, consulting, and global industry. Over the course of her career, she has worked across seven different Australian federal government agencies, as well as with major organisations including Deloitte, PwC, the Difference, and BP. This breadth of experience has given her a rare perspective across policy, public services, business, and complex organisational systems.  
Her work sits at the intersection of service design, strategy, systems thinking, and UX, with a strong focus on helping organisations navigate complexity and create meaningful, human-centred outcomes. She is known for bringing clarity to ambiguity, connecting people and ideas across silos, and translating strategic intent into practical action. Across different sectors and settings, Jing has built a reputation for thoughtful leadership, strong design judgement, and the ability to create long-term impact through design.  
In addition to her professional work, Jing is also an ADP List mentor and co-founder of the Australian Chinese Designers Association, where she supports ethnic Chinese designers across Australia and New Zealand. She is deeply committed not only to advancing design practice within organisations, but also to contributing to the wider design community through mentoring, leadership, and cross-cultural connection.  
Her work has been recognised through the Good Design Australia Awards, including a 2024 Green Tick in the Design Research category, and in 2025, a Gold Tick and Best in Class in the Design Strategy category. These achievements reflect both the quality of her work and the strategic impact she continues to create across sectors.

[[Rosie Mills - Bio]]

Presentation length: 20 mins

**When every role is being disrupted, the designers who lean in and shape the change will be the ones who define what comes next.  

AI is throwing the design industry into chaos – new tools every week, accelerating delivery, blurring boundaries between disciplines, and a media landscape full of noise and fear. But chaos isn't the end of the story. Drawing on complexity theory and her own design journey, Rosie Mills makes the case that disruption is where designers have always done their best work — and that the ones who stay curious, act boldly, and move with intent will be the ones who shape what design looks like on the other side.

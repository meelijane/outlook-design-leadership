## Design's bright future

- AI is bringing every single person closer to customers by reducing the time from idea to impact
- Designers have always been deeply connected to the technology they design for; now they can manipulate it directly
- The new wave of tooling will bring with it an opportunity to influence and shape process, principles and practice
- Design leaders are uniquely positioned to drive the future with their combination of attention to detail, taste, systems thinking and strategic prowess
- We don't know what the future is going to look like, but the people in this room will be helping to shape it

## Resilient leadership
- Leaders in industry now have led teams through COVID lockdowns, actual wars, terrorist attacks and multiple rounds of disruption in tooling
- Personal stories
	- Leading a team while Melbourne suffered through ongoing lockdowns, anxiety, fear and panic were just things I had to manage
	- Had a team member in the shopping centre in Bondi Junction when the stabbing happened; compassion, kindness and flexibility were critical
- Leadership in smooth waters is easy; leaders are tested and show their talents when the sea gets choppy
- Design leaders are grappling with a number of challenges today, but of course the most pressing one for many of us is AI
